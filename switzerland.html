Switzerland

Switzerland is a beautiful country known for its stunning Alps, clear lakes, and scenic landscapes. It is famous for Swiss chocolates, luxury watches, and efficient public transport. The country has four national languages: German, French, Italian, and Romansh. Switzerland is known for its neutrality and hosts many international organizations. Tourist spots like Zurich, Geneva, Interlaken, and Lucerne attract visitors from all over the world.
